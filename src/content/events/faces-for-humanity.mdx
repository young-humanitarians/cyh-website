For the second edition of our FACES FOR HUMANITY, we united outside the MSF (Médecins Sans Frontières) office in Geneva to have inspiring humanitarian discussions with special speakers. We were joined by Mr Abraham Doblado, Security and Crisis Management Capacity Building Coordinator at the International Committee of the Red Cross, and Ms Sitara Jabeen, Strategic Communications Advisor for Humanitarian Emergencies at MSF. This was also the official launch of our Geneva Hub!
