This year's Humanitarian Challenge is based on a simple idea: running together is a visible act of solidarity, and the starting point for something bigger. Run for Humanity, the CYH's Humanitarian Challenge 2026, brings a community run, an introduction to the Geneva Conventions, and hands on Skill Activation booths into one afternoon, in partnership with the Geneva Conventions' World Tour.
