How can “Glühwein” become even more delicious in the cold winter months? The Circle of Young Humanitarians (CYH) is teaming up with the wine shop Vinarius to warm up even more than our hearts and hands. Thanks to Vinarius, you can enjoy delicious “Glühwein” for a good cause: of every glass, the profit is donated to the CYH to help us fulfill our goal of bridging the humanitarian world to the next generation - so, every sip of Glühwein will be making a meaningful difference.
