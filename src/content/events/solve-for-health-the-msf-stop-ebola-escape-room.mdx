Our 2024 Humanitarian Challenge invited participants to step into the shoes of an Médecins Sans Frontières (MSF) medical team battling an Ebola outbreak. This immersive escape room experience developed by MSF not only aimed to raise awareness about challenges faced by these medical teams, but enabled attendees to dive into the humanitarian world in a more tangible way. Thank you to all participants for taking the time to come by, solve the challenge, mingle and connect!
