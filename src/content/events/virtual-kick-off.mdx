During the first part of our journey to the humanitarian world, we got to know the work of the International Committee of the Red Cross (ICRC) by hearing from two inspiring ICRC delegates located in Syria and in Jerusalem to learn first-hand what it really means to be a humanitarian. With over 180 participants, we were happy to give a first glimpse into the Circle of Young Humanitarians and what the future has in store for us.
