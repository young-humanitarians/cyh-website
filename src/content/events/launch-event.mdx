The Circle of Young Humanaritarians has officially been launched! We celebrated the launch of the CYH with delicious food and drinks, a great musical performance and good vibes with all of the participants. During the launch event, the participants got to know how the International Committee of the Red Cross (ICRC) helps relieve the suffering of people affected by armed conflict through a discussion with ICRC delegate Mark Kessler and learned what the Circle is really all about.
