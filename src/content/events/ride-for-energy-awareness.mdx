For people affected by armed conflicts, the lack of energy security becomes a question of survival. For example, during the 1991 Gulf War, the majority of deaths were not caused by the direct impact of bombs, but by the destruction of the electric power grid that led to the collapse of the public health, water and sanitations systems. We dedicated our 2022 Humanitarian Challenge to raise awareness on the value of energy and its role as a key to survival in armed conflicts
