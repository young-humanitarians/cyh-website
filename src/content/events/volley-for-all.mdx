Volley For All – a collaboration between the Circle of Young Humanitarians, SPORTEGRATION and Swiss Volley – was a fantastic day of volleyball and community. Participants had a great time training with Swiss national players, connecting with new people, and competing in friendly matches. A big thank you to everyone who joined and helped raise funds to support the work of SPORTEGRATION and CYH!
