During our first Humanitarian Challenge «Sweat for Change» we offered a sport class or partnered up with some of our favorite sport providers in Zurich each day so that every sweat drops makes a meaningful difference: with over 90 participants, the Circle of Young Humanitarians was able to donate just over 3000 CHF to the International Committee of the Red Cross! A special thank you goes to our amazing partners Mix Studio, The Loft by LH Personaltraining, Lucky Punch, Kampfkunst Zürich and Kathrin Schweizer – thank you for the great collaboration!
