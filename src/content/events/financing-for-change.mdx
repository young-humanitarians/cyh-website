At a time where humanitarian assistance is increasingly needed, what happens when even leading humanitarian organizations cannot fund their vital work? Our second Humanitarian Enrichment event “Financing for Change” took place on July 26, 2023 and welcomed experts from the International Committee of the Red Cross (ICRC), Médecins Sans Frontières (MSF), Save the Children and many more for an evening of critical discussions with invaluable perspectives on financing the humanitarian world.
