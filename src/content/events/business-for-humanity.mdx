Drawing on the new HSG course “Social Responsibility: Humanitarian Principles in the Corporate Context”, co-created and co-taught by Dr. Andreas Boehm, Mahmoud Haidar & Kay von Mérey (Co-Founder & President, CYH), we hosted a public CEMS and MIA lecture with Dr. Peter Maurer, President of the Basel Institute on Governance and former President of the International Committee of the Red Cross (ICRC). At the University of St. Gallen, the event brought together students and professionals for dialogue on humanitarian principles in business.
