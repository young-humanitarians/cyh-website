Our 2023 Humanitarian Challenge, in collaboration with the Open Your Eyes Photo Festival in Zurich, aimed to raise awareness about the United Nations's 17 Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). The “OPEN YOUR EYES” campaign turned Zurich into a visual museum with creative photography, engaging the people in critical global issues. As part of our Humanitarian Challenge, the Circle of Young Humanitarians took over the festival's visitor center at General-Guisan-Quai and challenged the public to join for a volunteer shift. Thank you to everyone who stopped by and participated!
