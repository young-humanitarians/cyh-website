Join us for an enchanting winter gathering where the Circle of Young Humanitarians (CYH) and Vinarius wine shop team up once again for a good cause. Enjoy the delicious “Glühwein” and support CYH's mission - bridging the humanitarian world to the next generation - as part of the proceeds for each glass sold goes directly towards fulfilling our goal. We invite you to come together with us to make a positive impact. Let's warm hearts and make a difference this season!
