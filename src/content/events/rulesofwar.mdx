Even wars have rules. What does that mean? We organized an intergenerational and interdisciplinary dialogue with inspiring speakers from Ukraine, the University of Zurich and the International Committee of the Red Cross (ICRC), to discover how the #RulesOfWar help people affected by armed conflict and other situations of violence and what they have to do with the mandate and mission of the International Committee of the Red Cross (ICRC).
