The most important set of rules in international humanitarian law (IHL), the Geneva Conventions from 1949, seek to limit the effects of armed conflict by regulating the conduct of hostilities and protecting the victims of armed conflict. Despite their vital global relevance, they face significant challenges in today's world. Our third Humanitarian Enrichment Forum offered a glimpse of life in war through Waad al-Kateab's courageous and powerful documentary FOR SAMA, as well as discussions about challenges and hopes for IHL with Dr. Anne Quintin (ICRC) and Dr. Hamza al-Kateab (Action For Sama Foundation).
