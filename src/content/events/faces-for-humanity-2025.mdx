In Sihlcity, Zurich, we unveilled the many faces of changemakers across communities, captured by the legendary Beat Mumenthaler during the Young Humanitarian Summit 2025. For our fourth Humanitarian Enrichement Forum, we partnered with the Inside Out Project, the largest global participatory art installation. We also had the presence of Achille Després, Spokesperson & Public Communication Advisor at International Committee of the Red Cross, who shared his experiences from his time in Ukraine and Afghanistan with us.
