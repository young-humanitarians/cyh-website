The CYH Humanitarian Wayfinder is a practical guide designed to help you visualize and embark on
the inner humanitarian work essential for cultivating a deeper sense of self-agency.

This guide includes the CYH Theory of Change, a foundational framework that outlines how cultural
transformation becomes possible. The Wayfinder is just one of many ways to put this theory into
action. We break down each step into a series of habits that can be developed and strengthened
over time. Additionally, we explore the different scales of inner humanitarian work and provide
practical tools and skills to support your personal journey.

This guide is a living document - continuously evolving as we learn and grow. We invite you to
engage with it, adapt it to your own experience, and join us in shaping a more just, beautiful,
brave, and empowered world.
