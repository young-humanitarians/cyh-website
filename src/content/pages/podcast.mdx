The "Young Humanitarian Podcast", created and hosted by the Circle of Young Humanitarians,
offers a platform for insightful conversations about the humanitarian world with experts from
diverse fields. The podcast emphasizes that the humanitarian field is interconnected with many
other disciplines, which is why we invite experts from diverse areas to showcase how everyone
can contribute. Further, it focuses on the importance of humanitarian work, current global
issues, and their relevance to the younger generation. Its goal is to raise awareness, share
knowledge, and foster dialogue between young people and the humanitarian sector. Based in
Zurich, Switzerland, the podcast also serves as an international touchpoint to connect with the
Circle of Young Humanitarians, offering guidance on how we can actively engage in society.
