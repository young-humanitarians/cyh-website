Our Advisory Board members offer guidance and expertise on various aspects, including overall
strategy, branding, networking, fundraising and personal development. The Board members of the
Circle of Young Humanitarians meet the members of the Advisory Board about three times a year to
discuss current challenges and opportunities and receive valuable advice and support. Esther
Schaufelberger heads the Advisory Board for a term of two years.
