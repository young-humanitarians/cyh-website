Waad al-Kateab's documentary “We Dare to Dream” tells the powerful stories of five athletes who, after fleeing their home countries, competed as part of the Refugee Olympic Team at Tokyo 2020. Screened in Zurich at an event organized by the Circle of Young Humanitarians, the film highlights resilience, identity, and the shared humanity behind the headlines.
