In times of war and conflict, young people are increasingly struggling to navigate the ever-growing stream of online content. The Circle of Young Humanitarians' (CYH) initiative to bridge the humanitarian world to the next generation is founded on the belief that young people's perception of war today impacts the direction of our world in the future.
