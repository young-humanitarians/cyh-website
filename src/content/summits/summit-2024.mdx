workshopsHeading: Workshops
workshops:
- title: '«Beyond Borders: Leveraging Open Source Information for Humanitarian Response»'
  organisation: INTERNATIONAL COMMITTEE OF THE RED CROSS (ICRC)
  description: >-
    Join the ICRC's workshop to understand the transformative impact of Open Source Information
    (OSI) in humanitarian response. Explore publicly available data, discussing the implications
    of digital technologies, the increasing digitalization of conflict, and how OSI can empower
    local populations in humanitarian operations.
- title: '«Courage and Humanity: Navigating Extreme Decisions»'
  organisation: MÉDECINS SANS FRONTIÈRES (MSF)
  description: >-
    Embark on a journey with Dr. Tankred Stöbe, an experienced emergency doctor with MSF.
    Engage in an interactive session exploring decision-making in extreme situations, gaining
    deeper insights into the ethical dilemmas and complexities faced in crisis environments.
- title: «Immerse Yourself in the Swiss Humanitarian Aid Crisis Cell»
  organisation: SWISS AGENCY FOR DEVELOPEMENT & COOPERATION (SDC)
  description: >-
    Immerse yourself in the crisis cell of Swiss Humanitarian Aid. Gain insights into rapid
    responses, decision-making processes, and the challenges involved. Specialists lead this
    workshop, offering a concrete understanding of Switzerland's humanitarian contributions.
- title: '«Safeguarding the Rights of Refugees: A Role for the New Generation»'
  organisation: UNHCR
  description: >-
    With 108.4 million forcibly displaced worldwide, delve into legal and physical protection
    challenges faced by refugees. UNHCR's workshop explores global initiatives, discussing
    how the new generation can support displaced individuals, especially in Switzerland.
- title: «Response to Crisis in Switzerland»
  organisation: SWISS RED CROSS (SRC)
  description: >-
    Combat helplessness with action. Join the Swiss Red Cross to explore tangible ways of
    alleviating human suffering in Switzerland. This interactive workshop addresses self-care
    amidst tragic refugee fates, fostering empowerment and resilience.
- title: '«Learning Must Go On: How to Realize the Right to Education During Conflict?»'
  organisation: SAVE THE CHILDREN
  description: >-
    Recognize the pivotal role of education in humanitarian response. Join Save the Children's
    workshop to discuss effective practices and persistent challenges in ensuring the right
    to education for crisis-affected children.
- title: '«War, Fake News and Cellphone Videos: Investigating Sources from Conflict Zones»'
  organisation: NZZ
  description: >-
    Explore NZZ's methods and tools to acquire accurate insights from war zones. This workshop
    provides hands-on techniques for examining satellite imagery, statements, and cellphone
    videos from conflict parties, emphasizing the crucial role of accurate information in
    effective humanitarian responses.
- title: «Durable Solutions for Children Caught in Protracted Crises»
  organisation: UNICEF SCHWEIZ UND LIECHTENSTEIN
  description: >-
    In this UNICEF-led workshop, participants explore child-centered approaches to humanitarian
    action. Delve into the unique challenges faced by children in crises, discussing education,
    health, and protection. Discover how UNICEF works to ensure the rights and well-being
    of every child, inspiring contributions to a better future.

The Summit facilitated dynamic discussions, engaging the next generation alongside high-level representatives, industry leaders, and inspiring thought leaders, fostering interdisciplinary and intergenerational exchange.

Held under the patronage of the ICRC, the Circle of Young Humanitarians proudly presents the second Young Humanitarian Summit (YHS), a platform for fostering interdisciplinary and intergenerational exchange. The YHS 2024 focused on humanitarian assistance throughout the complexities of conflicts.

Over two days, we invited young people of all backgrounds to connect, learn from each other and exchange views with high-level speakers, diverse representatives and inspiring thought leaders from the humanitarian world.
